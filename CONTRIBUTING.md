# Contributing

This tool is a teaching resource for PHIL 2001, *Ethics and Evolutionary Games* (Northeastern University), and part of the course's open class project. Contributions are welcome from students in the course and from the wider community.

## How to contribute
- Open an issue to report a bug or suggest a feature.
- Fork the repository and open a pull request with your change.
- Students in PHIL 2001 may also contribute through the course's class-project process.

## Licensing of contributions
By submitting a contribution (a pull request, patch, or other material), you agree that it is licensed under the same [MIT License](LICENSE) that covers this project. You retain the copyright to your contribution.

## Attribution
Contributors are credited in the repository history and release notes. Substantial contributions may be acknowledged in the project's citation metadata.
